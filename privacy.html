Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Overview

Alinda Messages is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what happens when you use our Service and what information (if any) is processed.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Alinda Messages does not collect or process any of the following:

  • Personal information — name, email, phone number, address
  • Account data — no accounts or login system exists
  • Usage analytics — no tracking pixels, no analytics, no telemetry
  • IP addresses — not logged or stored by the application
  • Cookies — the app does not set any cookies
  • Device information — not accessed or collected
  • Geolocation — not accessed or used

2. Local Storage / Session Storage

The app uses sessionStorage on your device solely to remember whether you've passed the verification gate during your current browsing session. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device (in your browser)
  • Is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab or window
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Contains no personal information — just a boolean flag ("true")

3. Clipboard Access

When you tap the "Copy" button, the app uses your browser's Clipboard API to copy the selected message text to your clipboard. This action happens entirely on your device. No clipboard content is transmitted to any server.

4. Share Functionality

When you tap the "Share" button, the app uses your device's native share sheet (Web Share API) to let you send a message through apps of your choice (WhatsApp, SMS, email, social media, etc.). The sharing is handled by your device and chosen app — Alinda Messages does not intercept, log, or store what you share or whom you share it with.

5. Third-Party Services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may collect standard HTTP request logs (IP address, request headers) as part of its font delivery service. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy. No personal data from Alinda Messages is shared with Google.

The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process standard HTTP request data (IP address, request metadata) to serve the website. This is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy. Alinda Messages does not configure any additional Cloudflare analytics or tracking.

6. Children's Privacy

Alinda Messages is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The age verification gate ensures that users confirm they are 13 or older before accessing the Service. If you believe a child under 13 has accessed the Service, please note that no personal data was or could have been collected.

7. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored by the application, there is no risk of personal data breach from Alinda Messages. The only data stored is a session flag on your local device, which is automatically deleted when you close your browser.

8. Your Rights

Since Alinda Messages does not collect personal data, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) are not applicable. However:

  • You can clear the session verification flag at any time by closing your browser tab
  • You can use the Service without any persistent data being stored on your device
  • You can browse in private/incognito mode for zero local storage

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last updated" date. Since the app does not collect data, we do not anticipate any changes that would affect your privacy.

10. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Alinda Messages is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) by virtue of not collecting any personal data. There is no personal data to access, rectify, erase, or port.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please reach out through the Alinda Creation Hub platform.
